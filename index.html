tools built for execution, not decorations.

01 / ORDER FLOW

Footprint & CVD

Tick-level cluster analysis, volume profile, bid/ask delta imbalance, and cumulative volume delta calculated locally in microsecond loops.

02 / LIQUIDATIONS

2D Liquidation Heatmap

Real-time mathematical estimation of leverage liquidation bands (10x to 100x) directly from raw trade flows and open interest dynamics.

03 / AUCTION THEORY

TPO & Market Profile

30-minute letter brackets, Initial Balance, Value Area 70% (VAH/VAL), and POC with multi-period session merging and custom colors.

04 / REPLAY ENGINE

Tick Market Replay

High-fidelity historical replay with binary LZ4 cache. Step through volatile sessions tick-by-tick without internet lag.

05 / RISK MANAGEMENT

Trade Journal & PnL

Integrated trade log with instant chart autofill (Shift+G), risk-to-reward calculation, clipboard screenshot pasting, and local storage.

06 / SECURITY

Zero Telemetry

All API keys, notes, and workspace layouts stay 100% on your local drive. No middleman servers, no analytics, no tracking pixels.
